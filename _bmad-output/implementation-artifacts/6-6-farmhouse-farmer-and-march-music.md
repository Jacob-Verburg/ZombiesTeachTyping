# Story 6.6: Farmhouse, Farmer and March Music

Status: done

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a kid,
I want Horde Rush to look like a sunny farm with a funny farmer,
so that it feels like a new place to play.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Farmhouse + Farmer pair (FR58, NFR10, NFR13).** Every placeholder in Horde Rush is replaced by final art that follows `docs/art-style-sheet.md`: the lane backdrop (sky band + 5 lanes), the farmhouse on the right edge, the Farmer (idle, pacing walk, throw), the tomato in flight, a tomato splat on a hit, the hit flash, the melt, and the size classes (the player sprite scaled 1.0 / 1.25 / 1.5, as today). No `ColorRect` remains in `horde_rush_level.tscn` or `tomato.tscn`. Food and toys only: no guns, no weapon-shaped tools (the Farmer carries no pitchfork), no gore; defeat is a goofy green melt. Stamp red is never used (the "red" of the flash and the tomato is `pumpkin`, D16).
2. **Every new sheet passes the style tests.** Palette only, hard alpha, 1 px `ink` outline on characters and props, 2–6 frames at 8–12 fps (or tween-stepped like the poof), one ground row per sheet, Lossless import with no mipmaps. Each new sheet is listed in `test_art_sprites.gd` (sizes, frames), the art review scene and the style sheet table. Backdrop layers are exempt from the outline rule only and get their own checks (hard alpha, palette, no `candy-yellow`/`stamp-red`, and no `zombie-green`/`zombie-green-bright` inside the lanes so a copy never camouflages).
3. **The farmhouse fits the field.** The house front stands at `HOUSE_FRONT_X` (548) and every lane has its own doorway whose bottom sits on that lane's feet line (y 74, 118, 162, 206, 250), so an arriving copy visibly shuffles into its own lane's door (the 6.5 shuffle code is kept). The Farmer paces in front of the house at `DEFENDER_X` (520) and stays readable over the porch and lanes.
4. **The Farmer animates with the logic.** Idle before the first key and after the run ends; walk while pacing (the defender running); a one-shot throw on every throw, then back to walk. All driven from the logic steps (`HordeDefender.Step.thrown`, `on_run_started`, `on_run_ending`); nothing about the defender's timing, targeting or RNG changes.
5. **Hits, flash and melt use the art.** A tomato is the `tomato_fly` sheet; a landed hit spawns a self-freeing splat at the hit point; a non-final hit shows the copy's `flash` frames (the walk recoloured in palette, no runtime tint) for `hit_flash_s`; a final hit plays the `melt` frames (fall, slump, puddle) stepped by a tween over `melt_s`, then frees the copy. Hits, stops and brains still come only from logic (6.4/6.5 rules unchanged). With a stripped `SpriteFrames` (no `flash` / `melt`), nothing crashes (NFR16).
6. **The hat sits correctly on scaled copies.** On the medium (1.25) and brute (1.5) classes, through every frame of `walk`, `flash`, `melt`, `idle` and `dance`, the hat follows the anchors plus the copy's scale: a test proves the hat slot's seat is the body's per-frame head point under the copy's transform, and a 3× screenshot crop of each class marching with the Pumpkin hat shows the brim on the crown with no gap or overlap of more than the designed 1 px ink row (6.3 deferred item "6.6 owns the final art and hat fit on scaled classes").
7. **March music and sounds (FR58, NFR14).** A new march loop `mus_horde_rush` (OGG, looping, 60–120 s, goofy "rising pressure", never scary or babyish) plays through `AudioManager` because `horde_rush.tres` sets `music_id = &"mus_horde_rush"`. `sfx_zombie_spawn` plays on every spawned copy, `sfx_tomato_throw` on every throw, `sfx_tomato_hit` on a non-final hit, `sfx_melt` on a final hit, through a `play_sfx` seam (default `AudioManager.play_sfx`). All five files are generated by `tools/gen_audio.gd` (CC0 by authorship), each has exactly one row in `assets/audio/CREDITS.md`, and the mix rules hold (music under every effect but the wrong-key tick; the tick stays the quietest effect; burst cues throttled).
8. **Approval gates.** Smuck approves the new sheets (Gate 1, before they are wired), the in-game look (Gate 2) and the listen (Audio gate); each answer is recorded verbatim with the date in `## Art Approval` / `## Audio Approval`. The **Horde Rush field readability checklist** and the **Horde Rush listen checklist** (Dev Notes) are filled in pass/fail.
9. **No regressions.** Words, lanes, seeds, the march, the defender, hits, arrivals, brains, the +25 bonus, the outro and the hitch cap behave exactly as after 6.5 (only the visuals and sounds change). The existing MVP audio files are byte-identical after regenerating. Horde Rush stays `available = false`. Full GUT suite green.

### Design decisions made for this story (follow them; record any change and why)

- **Flash = recoloured frames, not a tint.** 6.4's `HIT_FLASH_MODULATE` creates off-palette pixels and was explicitly a placeholder ("Story 6.6 owns the final red-flash effect"). Stamp red is reserved (D16), so the "red" is `pumpkin`: a `flash` sheet that is `walk` with a legend swap (same mask, same ink, same anchors). PlayerZombie switches walk↔flash keeping the frame index, so the march never stutters. The hat does not flash (fine: the zombie flashes).
- **Melt = a tween-stepped sheet.** `melt_s` is a tuning number (6.7 may change it), so the 6 frames are stepped by a tween across `melt_s` (the villager poof precedent, style sheet: "frames stepped by the poof's tween") instead of a fixed fps. At 0.6 s that is 10 fps, inside the 8–12 rule. The hat rides down on the melt anchors and ends on the puddle (cute, intentional).
- **Splat = its own self-freeing one-shot** under `%Effects`, spawned at the logical landing point; it never gates input or logic.
- **Farmer = a level-local character** (`scenes/levels/horde_rush/`, sheets in `assets/sprites/characters/farmer/`). It mirrors `PlayerZombie`'s small play API, not its class (no hat, no hop). Faces left only (it paces vertically; no flip).
- **Backdrop = two static layers** (`field.png`, `farmhouse.png`) in `assets/sprites/backdrops/farmhouse/` (one folder per house+defender pair, so Epic 10 adds `castle/` and `beach_hut/`). They do not scroll, so there is no seam rule and no backdrop script; plain `Sprite2D`s.
- **A door per lane.** Copies arrive in all five lanes at x 548, so the house's field-facing wall has five openings; one front door alone (the placeholder) would make four lanes walk into a wall.
- **Sound ids are tomato-specific** (`sfx_tomato_throw`, `sfx_tomato_hit`); Epic 10 adds its own per pair (deferred note). `sfx_zombie_spawn` and `sfx_melt` are pair-independent.
- **The march is a separate render path** in `gen_audio.gd` so `mus_menu` and `mus_zombie_run` stay byte-identical; only the new WAV is encoded.

### Architecture and rules to follow

- Godot **4.7.2** (standard), Compatibility renderer, GDScript only, GUT **9.7.1**. Static typing everywhere (`untyped_declaration` is an Error), typed dictionaries/arrays/loop vars. Tabs. Short `##` docs that say why. LF line endings for new text files.
- "Logic leads, visuals chase": animations, splats and sounds react to logic results (`HordeField.advance` arrivals, `HordeDefender.Step.thrown/landed`), never to sprite positions; one-shots self-free and never gate input; never `await` in level callbacks.
- No GDD number as a literal: `hit_flash_s`, `melt_s`, crossing times etc. stay in `horde_rush.tres`. Look values (frame counts, fps, the splat timing) may be script consts with a doc saying so.
- Boundaries: the level never reads input, the clock or `PlayerData`, never imports another level's scenes (assets may be shared; `villager_wave.png` is no longer used here); it may call `AudioManager` (through seams).
- Entities: `preload` scenes as consts, `setup()`/position/scale before `add_child`, free when done. No pooling (6.7 decides).
- Art: code-authored pixel art only, via the tool; palette only; no gradients, glows, soft shadows or anti-aliasing; `candy-yellow` is focus-only, `stamp-red` stamp-only.
- Audio: OGG music (looping), 16-bit mono WAV SFX normalized to the tool's `FILE_PEAK`, no leading silence, start/end at zero (`test_effect_source_files_follow_the_spec` checks this for every id in `SFX_IDS`).
- Logging: nothing per frame; a `Log.debug` per throw/hit is acceptable (6.4 logs stops at debug).
